# AVAM&M Credits

AVAM&M is an unofficial, non-commercial fan re-implementation of the *style* of classic 1980s
first-person party RPGs, built with Avalonia UI and .NET. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or connected to** any publisher or rights holder of the games that inspired it, and contains none of
their code, data, maps, text, graphics, music or sounds. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
